# Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how Personally Identifiable Information (PII) should be collected, processed, and protected.

---

## Examples of PII

- Full Name
- Date of Birth
- Passport Number
- Aadhaar Number
- PAN Number
- Driving License
- Email Address
- Mobile Number
- Bank Account Number

---

## Collection

Only collect information required for legitimate business purposes.

---

## Storage

PII must be stored only in approved databases with encryption enabled.

---

## Access

Access to PII is restricted to authorized employees.

---

## Sharing

PII must never be shared externally without customer consent unless legally required.

---

## AI Systems

PII should be masked before sending information to AI models.

Example:

Original:

Rahul Sharma

Masked:

R***** S*****

---

## Logging

Sensitive information should never appear in application logs.

---

## Compliance

The company follows applicable privacy regulations and internal compliance policies.
