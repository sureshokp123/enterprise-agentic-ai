# Data Privacy Policy

## Objective

The company is committed to protecting customer and employee information.

---

## Personal Data

Personal information includes:

- Name
- Address
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Aadhaar Number
- PAN Number
- Bank Details

---

## Data Classification

Information is classified as:

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

---

## Access Control

Employees may access only the data required for their job responsibilities.

---

## Data Sharing

Confidential information must not be shared with external organizations without authorization.

---

## Encryption

Sensitive information must be encrypted during storage and transmission.

---

## AI Usage

Confidential customer information must never be uploaded to public AI tools.

Only approved enterprise AI platforms may be used.

---

## Data Retention

Business records should be retained according to legal and regulatory requirements.

---

## Violations

Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information may result in disciplinary action.
