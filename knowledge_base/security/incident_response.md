# Security Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the process for responding to security incidents.

---

## Examples of Security Incidents

- Data Breach
- Malware Infection
- Ransomware
- Credential Theft
- Unauthorized Access
- Lost Company Laptop
- Insider Threat

---

## Incident Severity

Critical

High

Medium

Low

---

## Reporting Process

Employee

↓

Manager

↓

Information Security Team

↓

Investigation

↓

Resolution

---

## Response Time

Critical incidents should be reported immediately.

High severity incidents within one hour.

Medium severity incidents within four hours.

---

## Investigation

The security team will:

- Collect evidence
- Preserve logs
- Identify affected systems
- Determine root cause
- Recommend corrective actions

---

## Recovery

Systems should be restored only after verification by the security team.

---

## Lessons Learned

Every major incident must include a post-incident review to prevent recurrence.
