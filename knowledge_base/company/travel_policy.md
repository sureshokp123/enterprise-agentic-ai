# Travel Policy

## Business Travel

Business travel must receive prior manager approval.

International travel requires Director approval.

---

## Flight Booking

Employees should choose economy class for domestic travel.

Business class requires executive approval.

---

## Hotel Booking

Maximum hotel reimbursement:

Domestic:
₹6000 per night

International:
According to destination guidelines.

---

## Taxi

Ride-sharing services are preferred.

Luxury vehicle reimbursement is not permitted without approval.

---

## Meals

Meal reimbursement:

Breakfast: ₹500

Lunch: ₹800

Dinner: ₹1000

Actual bills must be submitted.

---

## Local Transport

Metro

Taxi

Bus

Rental Car (Manager Approval Required)

---

## Expense Submission

Travel expenses must be submitted within 7 days after travel completion.

---

## Required Documents

Travel Ticket

Hotel Invoice

Taxi Receipt

Meal Bills
