# Company Policy

## About Our Company

Enterprise AI Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a technology company specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Full Stack Development, and Enterprise Software Solutions.

Our mission is to build secure, scalable, and intelligent software that helps organizations automate business processes using AI.

---

# Working Hours

Monday to Friday

Working Hours:
9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Lunch Break:
1:00 PM - 2:00 PM

Employees are expected to work 8 productive hours per day.

---

# Hybrid Work Policy

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week.

Managers may require employees to attend office meetings whenever business needs require.

---

# Code of Conduct

Employees should:

- Respect colleagues.
- Maintain professionalism.
- Protect confidential information.
- Follow company security policies.
- Avoid sharing credentials.
- Report unethical activities immediately.

---

# Communication

Official communication must happen using:

- Microsoft Teams
- Outlook Email
- Jira
- Confluence

Personal messaging applications should not be used for business discussions.

---

# Confidentiality

All company documents, source code, customer information, API keys, passwords, and infrastructure details are confidential.

Employees are prohibited from sharing internal information outside the organization.

---

# Equipment Policy

Company laptops remain company property.

Employees are responsible for maintaining company devices.

Lost devices must be reported within 24 hours.

---

# AI Usage Policy

Employees may use approved AI tools including:

- Azure OpenAI
- GitHub Copilot
- Internal AI Assistant

Sensitive customer information must never be shared with public AI services.

---

# Security

Multi-factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory.

Passwords must comply with company password policy.

VPN must be used while accessing production environments.

---

# Violations

Violation of company policies may result in:

- Warning
- Suspension
- Employment termination
