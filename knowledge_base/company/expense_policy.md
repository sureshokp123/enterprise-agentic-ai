# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses

Business Travel

Hotel

Meals

Internet

Office Supplies

Training

Conference Registration

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal Shopping

Entertainment

Alcohol

Family Expenses

Luxury Purchases

Traffic Fines

---

## Submission Timeline

Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days.

Late submissions require Finance approval.

---

## Required Documents

Original Invoice

GST Invoice

Manager Approval

Proof of Payment

---

## Reimbursement Process

Employee

↓

Manager Approval

↓

Finance Verification

↓

Payment Processing

↓

Bank Transfer

---

## Payment Timeline

Approved reimbursements are processed within 10 business days.
