# Payroll Policy

## Salary Payment

Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

---

## Salary Components

Basic Pay

House Rent Allowance

Special Allowance

Performance Bonus

Provident Fund

Professional Tax

Income Tax

---

## Payslips

Employees can download payslips from the HR Portal.

---

## Tax Declaration

Employees must submit tax declarations before December.

---

## Bonus

Performance bonuses are paid annually based on performance ratings.

---

## Overtime

Only approved overtime qualifies for additional payment.

---

## Payroll Queries

Payroll-related questions should be raised through the HR Helpdesk.
