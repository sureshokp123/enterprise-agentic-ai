# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Each employee receives 24 paid annual leave days every calendar year.

Unused leave can be carried forward up to 10 days.

---

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 paid sick leave days.

Medical certificates are required if leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.

---

## Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 casual leave days annually.

Casual leave should be requested at least one day in advance whenever possible.

---

## Maternity Leave

Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave according to company policy.

---

## Paternity Leave

Eligible employees receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave.

---

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be approved by the reporting manager.

Supporting documentation may be requested.

---

## Leave Approval Process

---

## Applying for Leave

Leave requests must be submitted using the HR Portal.

Email requests are accepted only during system outages.

---

## Public Holidays

The official holiday calendar is published every January.

Regional holidays depend on employee location.
