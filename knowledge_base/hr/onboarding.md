# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

The onboarding process helps new employees become productive by providing the required resources, training, and company information.

---

## Day 1 Activities

- Laptop Allocation
- ID Card Issuance
- Email Account Setup
- Microsoft Teams Access
- HR Orientation
- Manager Introduction

---

## First Week

Employees should complete:

- Security Awareness Training
- Code Repository Access
- Development Environment Setup
- Project Introduction
- Team Meetings

---

## First Month

Employees are expected to:

- Complete mandatory training.
- Understand company policies.
- Participate in sprint planning.
- Deliver their first assigned task.

---

## Required Documents

- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Bank Account Details
- Educational Certificates
- Previous Employment Documents

---

## IT Setup

Every employee receives:

- Company Laptop
- VPN Access
- Outlook Email
- Microsoft Teams
- GitHub Account
- Jira Access
- Confluence Access

---

## Probation

New employees remain on probation for six months.

Performance is reviewed before confirmation.
