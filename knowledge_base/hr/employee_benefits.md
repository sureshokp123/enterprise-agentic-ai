# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance

The company provides medical insurance for employees.

Eligible dependents may also be covered.

---

## Provident Fund

PF contributions follow Government regulations.

Both employee and employer contribute every month.

---

## Gratuity

Employees completing five years of continuous service become eligible for gratuity.

---

## Learning Benefits

Employees receive:

- Online Learning Platforms
- Technical Certifications
- Conference Sponsorship
- Internal Workshops

---

## Wellness Benefits

- Annual Health Checkup
- Mental Wellness Support
- Employee Assistance Program

---

## Internet Allowance

Employees working remotely receive a monthly internet reimbursement.

---

## Flexible Working

Eligible employees may choose hybrid work arrangements based on business needs.
