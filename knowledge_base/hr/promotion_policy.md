# Promotion Policy

## Objective

Promotions recognize employees who consistently demonstrate outstanding performance, leadership, and technical expertise.

---

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible after completing at least 18 months in their current role.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Promotion decisions consider:

- Performance Rating
- Technical Skills
- Communication
- Leadership
- Customer Feedback
- Innovation
- Mentoring

---

## Performance Ratings

Outstanding

Exceeds Expectations

Meets Expectations

Needs Improvement

---

## Promotion Process

Employee

↓

Manager Recommendation

↓

Department Review

↓

HR Approval

↓

Promotion Letter

---

## Salary Revision

Salary revisions are based on:

- Market Standards
- Internal Equity
- Performance Rating
- Budget Availability

---

## Promotion Cycle

Promotions are normally reviewed once every year.
