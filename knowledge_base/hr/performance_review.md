# Performance Review Policy

## Review Frequency

Performance reviews occur twice every year.

Mid-Year Review

Annual Review

---

## Evaluation Areas

Technical Skills

Quality

Productivity

Problem Solving

Communication

Teamwork

Leadership

Innovation

---

## Rating Scale

5 - Outstanding

4 - Exceeds Expectations

3 - Meets Expectations

2 - Needs Improvement

1 - Unsatisfactory

---

## Goal Setting

Employees define measurable goals with their reporting managers.

Goals should follow the SMART principle.

---

## Feedback

Managers provide:

- Positive Feedback
- Improvement Areas
- Career Guidance
- Training Recommendations

---

## Improvement Plan

Employees receiving low ratings may enter a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

The plan normally lasts 60 to 90 days.
