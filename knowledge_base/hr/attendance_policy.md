# Attendance Policy

## Working Days

Monday to Friday

---

## Office Hours

9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Employees should complete eight working hours each day.

---

## Attendance Recording

Attendance is recorded using the HR Portal.

---

## Late Arrival

Employees arriving more than 30 minutes late repeatedly may receive manager feedback.

---

## Work From Home

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

---

## Absence

Unplanned absence should be reported before the start of working hours.

---

## Overtime

Overtime requires prior manager approval.

---

## Holidays

Public holidays follow the company holiday calendar.

Regional holidays depend on office location.

---

## Attendance Violations

Repeated attendance violations may affect performance reviews.
